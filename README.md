# Embrace the frontier

**no/pace:** no deliberate slowdown or pause of civilian AI. 

**A community response to AI doomerism.** 

## The problem

Labs, activists, and some lawmakers want to pause, cap, or lock down frontier AI. They call it pacing. If that happens, most people lose access. Local models get restricted. APIs get locked to a few companies. Only labs that can pay for compliance keep building.

The public mostly hears that AI is dangerous, data centers are evil, and hard regulation is the only solution. They might hear that a model solved a fancy math problem, or that protein-folding AI won a Nobel Prize. But they don't hear about the real-world benefits of the technology, and they might not realize what they could lose if their government over-regulates AI. 

Safety is important, and regulation is not fundamentally evil. Like any technology, AI is dangerous in the wrong hands. But that isn't an argument for taking it away. 

We want local models, normal API access, and everyday use of frontier models to stay available. We don't want the frontier owned by one government or a handful of labs.
## Our approach

**Prove to the general public that AI is net-positive and not net-negative.** 

We'll collect real examples of AI helping ordinary people in tangible ways. 

We aren't going to convince the average voter to preserve AI by showing them use cases they can't relate to. We must showcase the very best applications of the technology to real-world problems.
